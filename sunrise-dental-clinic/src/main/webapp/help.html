How to use the system

1
Login

Open the staff portal and enter your username and password. Only authorized clinic staff can continue.

2
Register a new appointment

Choose New Appointment. Enter patient name, address, contact number, dentist, treatment, date, and time. The system assigns a unique number such as SDC-20260820-001 and blocks double bookings.

3
Display appointment details

Open Find Appointment and search by appointment number. The full patient record, dentist, treatment, date, and time appear on screen.

4
Calculate and print the bill

Open Calculate Bill, enter the appointment number, preview the consultation fee plus treatment cost, save the bill, then print the receipt.

5
Exit safely

Click Exit in the top-right corner. This logs you out and closes the session so the next staff member must sign in.
